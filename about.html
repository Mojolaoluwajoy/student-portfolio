About me

Hi, I'm Mojolaoluwa

I'm a Java Backend Engineer from Ogun State, Nigeria, and a Software Engineering student at MIVA Open University. I build secure, reliable backend systems with Spring Boot and PostgreSQL, mostly for fintech: banking platforms, wallets and payment services.

I've worked remotely with teams at Springcore Africa, Rova, Kanour Digital Services and Techservices, on projects ranging from corporate internet banking to vehicle tracking. My degree is helping me grow the parts of software I touch less often, like the front-end web technologies used to build this site.

Technical skills

  • Languages: Java, JavaScript (Node.js), SQL, HTML5, CSS3
  • Frameworks: Spring Boot, Spring Security, Spring Data JPA, Hibernate
  • Databases: PostgreSQL, MongoDB
  • APIs & security: REST API design, JWT, Keycloak, webhooks, Postman
  • Messaging: RabbitMQ
  • Tools: Git, GitHub, Maven, Docker, Liquibase, Flyway, Linux
  • Practices: Test-Driven Development, OOP, clean architecture, microservices

Career aspirations

I want to grow into a senior backend engineer and, eventually, a software architect who designs the payment and banking systems that millions of Africans rely on every day. I'm especially interested in making money movement safe: no double debits, no lost transfers, and a clear audit trail.

Hobbies & interests

  • Personal finance, investing and finding new ways to build income
  • Exploring how the apps and technology I use every day, from banking and payments to shopping and transport, work behind the scenes
